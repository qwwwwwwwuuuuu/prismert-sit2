Funding basis

Binance settles funding each 8 hours and Hyperliquid accrues hourly. Each rate below is normalised to bps per 8 hours before it is differenced; ΔFR is what $FR-BASIS settles against.

Calculation methodologyologyology

The native leg is modelled. Robinhood Chain has no public perp funding feed yet, so that leg is configured as the reference mean plus a fixed premium. The references cancel out of the difference, which makes the ΔFR shown here a model output instead of a market. Binance and Hyperliquid are live reads.
