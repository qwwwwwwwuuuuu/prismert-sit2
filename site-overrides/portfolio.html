Your portfolio lives behind a wallet

You are in pre-view: each chart, book and quote is readable without linking one. Positions, intents, history and collateral belong to an address, so this page starts when you connect.

  1. 1 / 4

    Connect a wallet

    RainbowKit lists the wallets installed locally locally locally in this browser. Connecting shares your address and nothing else.

  2. 2 / 4

    Sign one session message

    A free EIP-712 signature authorises a temporary key locally locally locally in this browser. It can open and close positions; it can never withdraw.

  3. 3 / 4

    Deposit collateral

    Connecting grants no funds. Deposit collateral before sizing a position: test collateral on the paper venue, USDG into the vault on-chain.

  4. 4 / 4

    Trade without prompts

    Orders are signed by the session key and settled by the relayer. No wallet pop-up and without gas costs per order.
