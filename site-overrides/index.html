Volatility and funding-rate derivatives

PUSHIN lists two markets that aren’t bets on price: 30-day crypto implied volatility, and the funding spread between Robinhood Chain and the major perpetual venues. Payouts are capped and set aside before execution, and the oracle is rate-limited on-chain.

What is priced, and what bounds it

Two indices computed from venue data, and three limits on the risk they carry. Each card opens the module behind it and says what state that module is in.

30-day implied volatility

Live

C-VIX is the fair strike of a 30-day variance swap, rebuilt each collection round from the Deribit and Derive BTC and ETH option surfaces.

Funding-rate basis

Live

FR-BASIS subtracts the Binance and Hyperliquid average from native funding, converts the result to an eight-hour basis and accrues it by the second.

8× payout cap

Tested

Profit is capped at eight times initial margin, and the whole cap is set aside from the payout pool when the position opens.

Oracle dampener

Tested

Settlement moves at most 20% in a block. A push beyond the band is distrusted and moves it 5% of the way, so a single faulty observation cannot reprice the book and a real move still arrives.

Skew borrow fee

Charged

Borrow costs rise quadratically for the crowded side as imbalance increases; the opposite side isn’t charged.

Risk controls below describe the reference protocol model. The contract model is a specification; PUSHIN has no deployed trading contracts.
