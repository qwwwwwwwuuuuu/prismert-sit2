Wallet and session

Connecting is the gateway from looking at PUSHIN to using it. A single signature authorises a session; after that, orders sign in the browser with without another wallet prompt and without gas costs.

Typed-data schema

From pre-view to a position

  1. 1Pre-view

    Live

    Each market, chart, book, funding table and risk panel is readable without a wallet. No address is collected and the ticket computes each figure.

  2. 2Link a wallet

    Live

    RainbowKit lists the EVM wallets installed locally locally locally in this browser, discovered over EIP-6963. Connecting shares your address and nothing else.

  3. 3Sign one session message

    Live

    A gas-free EIP-712 signature names a temporary key generated locally locally locally in this browser, its scope, your wallet's revocation epoch and a 12-hour expiry. The relayer verifies it and the header turns to Session verified; on a deployed venue the settlement contract verifies it again with each order.

  4. 4Deposit collateral

    Simulation venue

    Connecting grants nothing: an account starts at zero. On the paper venue USDG or ETH is credited as test collateral. On a deployed venue it is your wallet's own approve-and-deposit of USDG into PUSHINVault.

  5. 5Sign and open positions

    Simulation venue

    Each order is an EIP-712 intent signed by the session key: without another wallet prompt, without gas costs. The relayer verifies it, reserves margin, and fills it at the index.

  6. 6On-chain settlement

    Implemented · awaiting deployment

    The relayer submits signed intents to PUSHINSettlement.settleBatch, which verifies each one — the session grant behind it included — and settles it on its own. Built and rehearsed end to end on a local chain; waits on the testnet deployment.

The session key is created locally locally locally in this browser and stored in its localStorage under your address. Ending the session revokes it at the relayer and deletes it here. It never leaves this device.

Session key scope

The actions a temporary key may sign for you.

It can

  • Create a position using collateral that is already deposited.
  • Close a position you own.
  • Cancel an intent that hasn’t settled.
  • Increase or reduce test collateral only on the simulation venue. On-chain collateral may be moved solely by your wallet.

It can never

  • Send on-chain collateral to any destination address, including your own.
  • Transfer tokens, approve spenders or call other contracts.
  • Remain valid beyond its expiry or after End session is selected in the wallet menu.
  • Be replayed: order nonces are single-use and each other action is accepted once, by hash.

Scope as signed: trade:open trade:close trade:cancel paper:collateral

What you sign, and what you do not

Session grant
Your wallet signs this once as EIP-712 typed data: it is a message, not a transaction.
Orders, closes, cancels
Signed by the session key locally in the browser. Your wallet isn’t involved.
Gas
Trading itself requires without gas costs because orders, closes and cancellations are signed messages. The simulation venue is entirely gasless; on-chain, only deposits and withdrawals are wallet transactions, while the relayer funds order settlement.
Network
The signing domain uses Robinhood Chain, chain ID 4663. Because typed signatures are chain-specific, the wallet switches networks before signing.
How the session key is kept
The key remains locally locally locally in this browser's localStorage under your address and is never transmitted elsewhere.

Before you connect anything. The exchange remains in its pre-launch phase. Collateral on the paper venue is a test balance that carries no real-world value: there is no real deposit to make and no airdrop to claim, and this site never sells $PRP. On a deployed venue the only transactions this site asks your wallet for are a deposit into PUSHINVault, a withdrawal from it, and revoking your session keys — never a transfer anywhere else.
