Directional perpetuals concentrate exposure when positioning becomes one-sided: in a trend most open interest points the same way, and the pool is the counterparty to all of it. Volatility has buyers and sellers at the same time, and a funding spread has two ends by construction. What imbalance remains is priced by a borrow fee on the crowded side and bounded by the payout cap.

Roadmap

The current state of each stage, with completion defined by explicit criteria instead of calendar dates.

Full roadmap
  1. Implemented and operational

    The foundation includes a real-time index, a public simulation venue and a contract suite deployed and exercised on testnet.

    Shipped
  2. Harden the live venue

    The simulation venue is public; monitoring, protection and reproducibility come before on-chain settlement.

    Now
  3. Chain venue on testnet

    A public site settles orders through PUSHINSettlement on Robinhood Chain Testnet, while prismperp.trade remains a simulation venue.

    Next
  4. Contracts v2

    All functionality required by the mainnet contracts is completed before review, ensuring the audit covers the production code.

    Next
  5. Terminal and API

    Position-management tools enabled by the v2 contracts, together with a documented software interface.

    Next
  6. Audit and security

    An independent assessment of the frozen contracts plus the operational safeguards required before real funds are accepted.

    Later
  7. Mainnet

    Real USDG on Robinhood Chain, opened slowly.

    Later
  8. $PUSHIN utility

    Staking and governance follow the token-model specification and are financed by actual trading fees.

    Later
  9. Research

    Concepts that require either a full design or evidence before they enter the schedule.
